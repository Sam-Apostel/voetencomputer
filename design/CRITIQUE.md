**Asks:** (1) fix the craft defects; (2) make it crayon (variable width, wobble, uneven hatching) and Rounded everywhere; (3) make the Trends chart truthful (ends at 78,4, readings on both sides of the line, 78/79/80 labels, flat last two weeks, circle inside the margin); (4) Household in the direction's voice (no white cards, drawn scales, forget row, height row, shake hint); (5) fidelity on Body and Signature (drawn BMI band strip, older-composition label, recognition steps, four distinct signature frames with seven-segment hunting digits and a half-drawn tick, uncropped).

- *"Set UI text in SF Pro Rounded"*: it already is (the scan lists one family, SF Pro Rounded, on every screen). Rounded at 17 regular is subtle; round 2 moves row labels to medium so it shows.
